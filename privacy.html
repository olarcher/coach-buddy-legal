Coach Buddy

Privacy Policy

Draft. Last updated: June 1, 2026

Coach Buddy is a local-first football coaching app for tournament days. This draft policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

Data You Enter

Coach Buddy lets you enter tournaments, games, teams, players, lineup information, notes, timing settings, and related coaching data. This information is stored locally on your device.

Photos and Camera

If you choose to add player avatars, Coach Buddy may ask for access to your camera or photo library. Photos are used only for the avatar you select or take inside the app.

Notifications

Coach Buddy may request permission to send local notifications for substitution reminders and game-related timers. These notifications are created on your device.

Backups and File Sharing

Coach Buddy may let you export or import backup files. You control where exported backup files are saved or shared.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Coach Buddy does not contain advertising and is not intended to track you across apps or websites.

Crash and Update Services

App distribution and updates may use Apple and Expo services. These services may process limited technical information needed to deliver, update, or diagnose the app.
