1. Controller and Contact

The controller for this privacy policy is Oliver Larcher. For privacy questions, contact oliver.larcher@gmail.com.

No data protection officer has been appointed because there is currently no legal obligation to appoint one.

2. What the App Stores Locally

Coach Buddy lets you enter and manage football coaching information, including player names, positions, notes, player avatars, tournaments, game schedules, opponents, scores, game events, lineup and substitution state, timer settings, and app language settings.

This app data is stored in a local database on your device. Coach Buddy does not provide an account system or a cloud sync service for this data. I do not receive the coaching data you enter unless you deliberately send it to me, for example in a support request.

3. Player Data and Youth Teams

Player names, notes, photos, lineup history, and match statistics can be personal data. If you use Coach Buddy for a club, school, youth team, or another organized group, you are responsible for entering and using that data lawfully and for obtaining any required permission from players, parents, guardians, or the organization.

4. Photos, Camera, and Media Library

Coach Buddy may ask for camera or photo library permission when you take or choose a player avatar. These permissions are optional and controlled by your device settings. Selected avatar images are copied into app storage so they can be displayed inside Coach Buddy.

5. Notifications, Timers, Live Activities, and Widgets

Coach Buddy may request permission to send local notifications for substitution reminders and game timers. Timer information may also be shown through system features such as live activities, widgets, or lock screen views. These features are used to display information from the current game and do not create a Coach Buddy account or cloud profile.

6. Backups, Imports, and Sharing

Coach Buddy can export a backup file and import a backup file that you select. Backup files may contain the coaching records stored by the app. Treat backup files as confidential, especially if they contain player names, notes, photos, or youth-team information.

When you save, share, upload, email, or otherwise transfer a backup file, the destination service you choose, such as a file provider, cloud drive, email app, or messaging app, handles that file under its own terms and privacy policy.

7. App Updates and Technical Services

Coach Buddy uses Expo services for app builds and over-the-air update delivery. When the app checks for updates, Expo may process technical information such as device operating system information and randomized update tokens used to determine whether an update has been downloaded.

If you install Coach Buddy through an app store, the store operator processes data under its own privacy policy. This may include Apple for App Store distribution and Google for Google Play distribution. Store operators may process account, download, payment, crash, diagnostics, security, review, and device information. Coach Buddy does not control those store operator processing activities.

8. Support Requests

If you contact support by email, I process your email address, message content, and any information you choose to include, such as screenshots, device details, or backup files. Do not send sensitive team or player information unless it is necessary for support.

9. This Website

This website is hosted with GitHub Pages. The website does not include advertising cookies or analytics scripts. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses and related technical information for security and service operation.

11. Recipients and Processors

App data stored only on your device is not sent to me by Coach Buddy. Depending on how you use the app and website, data may be processed by:

  • GitHub Pages, for hosting this website.
  • Expo, for app update delivery and related technical services.
  • Your app store provider, such as Apple App Store or Google Play.
  • Your email provider and my email provider when you contact support.
  • Any third-party service you choose when sharing or storing backup files.

12. International Transfers

Some service providers, including GitHub, Expo, app store providers, and email providers, may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses, or other lawful transfer mechanisms provided by those service providers.

13. Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you edit it, delete it, reset the app data, or uninstall the app. Backup files remain wherever you save or share them until you delete them there.

Support emails are kept for as long as needed to handle the request and for reasonable follow-up, unless a longer retention period is required for legal reasons. Website and technical-service logs are retained by the respective service providers according to their own policies.

14. Your Rights

Subject to the GDPR requirements, you may have the right to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and objection to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time with effect for the future.

For local data stored only on your device, the most direct way to access, correct, export, or delete it is to use the app's edit, export, delete, reset, or uninstall features.

15. Complaints

You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state of your habitual residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement.

16. Automated Decision-Making

Coach Buddy does not use automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

17. Changes

This policy may be updated when the app, services, or legal requirements change. The date at the top shows the latest update.
