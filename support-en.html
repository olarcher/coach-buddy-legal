Before You Send a Support Request

Please include the app version, your device model, operating system version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful. Do not send player names, youth-team information, backup files, or photos unless they are necessary to understand the issue.

About Coach Buddy

Coach Buddy is a local-first football coaching app for organizing tournaments, games, players, lineups, scores, and substitution reminders. The app does not require an account and does not provide a cloud sync service for coaching data.
